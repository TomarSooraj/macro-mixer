🎛️ Macro Mixer

The economy on a mixing board. Nine dials — gold, bonds, inflation, the dollar and more — wired the way real markets behave. Slide any one and watch the rest respond. Hit Explain under a dial for the math and a plain-English version.

How it works

One factor drives at a time. Whatever slider you grab becomes “the cause”; all eight others recompute from neutral as its effects, rippling through 2nd- and 3rd-order links (e.g. tension → oil → inflation → yields). Presets can pin two drivers at once.

Under the hood it's a directed influence network solved by damped iteration — stable, never explodes, and directionally faithful to how these instruments correlate. It shows typical co-movement, not a forecast.

    Reality check.
    • This is a teaching model of typical relationships, not a prediction. Real markets are noisy and regime-dependent.
    • Links marked ⚡ can flip sign depending on the regime — e.g. stocks vs. yields move opposite in inflation scares but together in growth scares.
    • Gold really tracks real yields (nominal yield − inflation) — which is why it can rise even as rates climb, if inflation climbs faster.
    • Rate hikes cool inflation only with long lags, so that particular link is deliberately weak here.
    • “Geopolitical Tension” doubles as the market’s general fear / risk-off dial (used that way in the 2008 & COVID presets).
